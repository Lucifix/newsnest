How it works

A 60-second loop, from feed to front page.

It fetches the sources that are due, scores new stories in batches, and writes key points for the ones above your threshold.

  1. 01

    Fetch

    RSS, Atom and GitHub releases, de-duplicated by URL.

  2. 02

    Group

    The same story across outlets becomes one.

  3. 03

    Score

    Claude rates topic · importance · fit.

  4. 04

    Rank

    Score × freshness × what you like.

  5. 05

    Your feed

    Likes, opens and ratings flow back into the ranking.

Every batch

A small, cheap model with prompt caching does the scoring.

Rarely

A stronger model drafts your profile and the weekly suggestions.

Your call

Both models are set in config.yaml.

Privacy

What leaves your server, and what doesn't.

Stays on your machine

  • Your feed list
  • Your ratings, likes and reading history
  • The database: one SQLite file
  • No telemetry. NewsNest phones home to no one.

Goes to the Anthropic API, with your key

  • Headlines and snippets, for scoring
  • Article text, when you ask for key points

No key? Then nothing goes to Anthropic at all, and the feed shows newest first.

Quick start

Up and running in five lines.

You need Docker with Compose and, for the AI features, an Anthropic API key.

terminal 
mkdir newsnest && cd newsnest
curl -fsSLO https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Lucifix/newsnest/main/docker-compose.yml
curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Lucifix/newsnest/main/.env.example -o .env     # set NEWSNEST_PASSWORD and ANTHROPIC_API_KEY
mkdir -p config data
docker compose up -d
  1. 1

    Say where you live

    Your country and city tell Claude whose news is “big”.

  2. 2

    Add local news

    Pick the pack for your country, or add your own local feeds.

  3. 3

    Rate about 30 headlines

    Claude drafts your interests from them. That's the setup.

Open http://localhost:3095, log in with your password, and tap Set up my profile. The login is a single shared password, so put NewsNest behind an HTTPS reverse proxy before you expose it to the internet.

This pulls the published image for amd64 and arm64, so a Raspberry Pi works too: ghcr.io/lucifix/newsnest or lucifix/newsnest on Docker Hub. For a Portainer stack, or to build from source, see the README.
